Hold off on FR-0006, let's go back to FR-0005 first — I want to correct the callback/timeout logic.

Here's how it actually works:
- When the call to the external AH-IPP service succeeds, cmic-app pends the case by calling CMiC360 (CMiC360 manages the pend, not just a local state).
- After that, there are two possible callbacks, and only whichever arrives FIRST gets processed:
  - source=IPP: AH-IPP itself calls back once it has the real result. This normally arrives before the 2-minute limit.
  - source=Case360: CMiC360 calls back on its own when the pending time limit (2 minutes) expires — this is CMiC360's timeout firing, not AH-IPP responding.
- So source=Case360 isn't really "AH-IPP's result" — it's CMiC360 signaling "time's up, unpend now." That's why it doesn't process an IPP result and just returns early after validating.
